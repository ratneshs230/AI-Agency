## Our Story

Manish Kapoor Planning Consultants was founded with a singular vision: to create architecture that transcends the ordinary and enriches lives. Based in Vasant Kunj, we have spent 18 years transforming this vision into reality through thoughtful design and meticulous execution.

What began as a passion for creating meaningful spaces has evolved into a comprehensive architectural practice serving clients across Delhi NCR and beyond. Today, with 30+ completed projects spanning 129620 sq.ft, we continue to push boundaries while honoring the fundamental principles of good design.

Our journey has been marked by continuous learning, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. Each project we undertake is an opportunity to create something unique—a space that tells a story, serves a purpose, and stands the test of time.

## Design Philosophy

### Sustainable design that respects environment and culture

At Manish Kapoor Planning Consultants, we believe that great architecture emerges from a deep understanding of context, client needs, and the interplay between form and function. Our Modern Contemporary approach ensures that every project we deliver is:

- **Contextually Responsive**: Designed to harmonize with its surroundings
- **Functionally Excellent**: Optimized for how spaces are actually used
- **Aesthetically Refined**: Beautiful in its simplicity and detail
- **Sustainably Conscious**: Mindful of environmental impact
- **Technologically Current**: Leveraging latest tools and methods
