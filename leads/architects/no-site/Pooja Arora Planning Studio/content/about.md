## Our Story

Pooja Arora Planning Studio was founded with a singular vision: to create architecture that transcends the ordinary and enriches lives. Based in Chanakyapuri, we have spent 25 years transforming this vision into reality through thoughtful design and meticulous execution.

What began as a passion for creating meaningful spaces has evolved into a comprehensive architectural practice serving clients across Delhi NCR and beyond. Today, with 65+ completed projects spanning 299903 sq.ft, we continue to push boundaries while honoring the fundamental principles of good design.

Our journey has been marked by continuous learning, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. Each project we undertake is an opportunity to create something unique—a space that tells a story, serves a purpose, and stands the test of time.

---

## Design Philosophy

### Timeless architecture rooted in contextual sensitivity

At Pooja Arora Planning Studio, we believe that great architecture emerges from a deep understanding of context, client needs, and the interplay between form and function. Our Industrial Chic approach ensures that every project we deliver is:

- **Contextually Responsive**: Designed to harmonize with its surroundings
- **Functionally Excellent**: Optimized for how spaces are actually used
- **Aesthetically Refined**: Beautiful in its simplicity and detail
- **Sustainably Conscious**: Mindful of environmental impact
- **Technologically Current**: Leveraging latest tools and methods
